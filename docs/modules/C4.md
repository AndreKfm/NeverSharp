## Goal

Port from checker.ts (typescript-go: `relater.go`):

- the relations (`assignableRelation`, `comparableRelation`, `subtypeRelation`, `strictSubtypeRelation`, `identityRelation`) and their caches, `isTypeRelatedTo`, `checkTypeRelatedTo` with `structuredTypeRelatedTo`, `recursiveTypeRelatedTo` (maybe-stack, depth limits, `isDeeplyNestedType`), `propertiesRelatedTo`, `signaturesRelatedTo`, `indexSignaturesRelatedTo`, `typeArgumentsRelatedTo` with variance (`getVariances`), union/intersection relations, literal/enum/primitive relations, `isSimpleTypeRelatedTo`, weak types, excess property checks (`hasExcessProperties`);
- the error elaboration that produces the diagnostic chains (`reportRelationError`, `elaborateError`, `elaborateObjectLiteral`, `elaborateArrayLiteral` …, the head messages such as 2322, 2345, 2741, 2739, 2559 and their related information), as tsc prints them;
- the type operations that depend on relations: subtype reduction in unions (`removeSubtypes`), the intersection reductions C3 left out (never-reduction, `T & primitive`, redundant template literals), `isTypeIdenticalTo`, `compareTypesAssignable`, constraint checks of type arguments (2344), `checkTypeAssignableTo` / `checkTypeAssignableToAndOptionallyElaborate` entry points used by declarations and expressions.
