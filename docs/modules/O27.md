## Goal

cheaper identifiers with byte-identical results: `TextTable.Intern` is 3.3 % of all samples (about 7 % of the parse), `CreateIdentifier` 0.7 %. O5b made the hash word-wise and rejected a hash carried from the scanner because the scanner rescans speculated tokens; O11 has since removed most speculation (2.23 M → 0.13 M speculations on the tuning corpus), so re-evaluate that. Other ideas, not prescribed: a small direct-mapped cache of recent identifiers in front of the table, keywords and common identifiers pre-interned, avoiding a second pass over identifier characters between scanner and table, cheaper `CreateIdentifier` (flags, original keyword kind). Thread safety, the NoAlloc contract and the tool contract as after O26.
