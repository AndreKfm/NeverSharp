## Goal

A systematic pass over the hot paths of the NativeAOT build (scanner `Scan` and its helpers, `NextToken` and the parse helpers, the expression descent, statements and declarations, lists, node creation and the store): find calls to small methods that NativeAOT does not inline (from the disassembly of the published driver, e.g. with the linker map or a disassembler on the native image, or `DOTNET_JitStdOutFile`/`JitDisasm` equivalents for ILC), decide by measurement which to inline or restructure (`AggressiveInlining`, splitting cold paths into separate non-inlined methods, static tables instead of switch helpers, sealed/direct calls instead of interface or virtual dispatch), and keep only what measurably helps. Byte-identical results; thread safety, the NoAlloc contract and the tool contract as after O28. Code-generation settings in `eng/` stay as pre-registered.
