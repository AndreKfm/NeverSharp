# O10: side table of rare node fields — brief

Step O10 of Track O ([track-o-steps.md](../track-o-steps.md)), after the endpoint (later gains are reported next to G = 0.60, never instead of it). One step without variants: it replaces a data structure, it does not choose between designs. Written by the orchestrator before the attempt started. The standing rules of [README.md](README.md) apply (Track O follows pre-registration §5.4). Runs in parallel with step O23 (scanner), which changes only `Scanner/`; do not touch the scanner.

## Starting point

`main` after O9 (commit ca06679). Fields that are undefined on almost every node of their class live in the store's side table instead of the record (`SIDE_FIELDS` / `SIDE_IN` in `tools/NeverSharp.Generate/emit-optimized.mjs`, chosen from the tuning corpus: set on fewer than about 3 % of the nodes of a class), plus the base fields `parent`, `original`, `modifierFlagsCache`, `emitNode` (`Arena/NodeStore.cs`). The side table is a `Dictionary<long, SideValue>` per store with a `long[]` log (`AddSide`, `SetSideInt`, `SetSideRef`, `GetSide*`).

The profile after O9 ([track-o-profile.md](../track-o-profile.md), last section; vscode, so for orientation only) shows about 6 % of the samples under `NodeStore.AddSide`: `Dictionary` growth (`Resize`) and the GC polls under it, reached from `CreateParameterDeclaration`, `CreateBasePropertyAccessExpression`, `CreateArrowFunction`, `CreateCallExpression`.

## Goal

1. A side table without per-file allocation and growth churn: for example an open-addressing table or an append-only entry log in per-thread memory (as O9's slabs), owned by the result like the other node data, with lookups no slower than today's. Its contents stay valid after the parse and independent of later parses.
2. The placement of fields (side table or record) may be revised only with data from the tuning corpus (pre-registration: the benchmark repositories are never used for a choice). If you change it, measure the per-class fill rates on the tuning corpus with a scratch tool, state the threshold, and record the table in your report.
3. Behaviour byte-identical (Gate 1), thread safety and the tool contract as after O9.
